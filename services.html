METHODOLOGY & DISCIPLINE

Our Process

A structured, transparent methodology refined over 525+ spatial projects — ensuring creative ambition aligns seamlessly with precision delivery.

01 PHASE 01

Discover

Understanding brand objectives, spatial constraints, visitor audience, and venue technical parameters.

OUTPUT: Design Brief & Spatial Framework
02 PHASE 02

Concept

Exploring spatial layouts, flow diagrams, architectural moodboards, and preliminary volumetric forms.

OUTPUT: 2D Spatial Plans & Mood Direction
03 PHASE 03

Design

Developing high-fidelity 3D visualizations, material palettes, lighting schemes, and structural blueprints.

OUTPUT: Photorealistic 3D & Material Specs
04 PHASE 04

Develop

Engineering fabrication drawings, CNC toolpaths, procurement schedules, and pre-build workshop mockups.

OUTPUT: Production CAD & Workshop Build
05 PHASE 05

Execute

Full-scale on-site assembly, electrical rigging, graphic installation, quality audit, and white-glove handover.

OUTPUT: Commissioning & On-Site Support
